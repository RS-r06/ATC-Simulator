How to play

You are the radar controller for Example Airport. Land every arrival on runway 27 and send every departure out of the sector by its exit fix, above 5000 ft. Keep aircraft 3 nm or 1000 ft apart.

Click an aircraft to select it, then type instructions and press Enter. You can give several at once: H180 A40 S210.

H270fly heading 270
L090 R090turn left or right onto 090
A40climb or descend to 4000 ft (2000 to 15000)
S210speed 210 kt
D KILNOfly direct to a fix
ILScleared for the ILS approach to runway 27
Tcleared for takeoff (departures at the runway)

To land, the easy way: D KILNO A30 ILS. The arrival flies to KILNO, turns onto the dashed centre line and descends by itself. The quick way: give it a heading that cuts the centre line 10 to 15 nm out, at 3000 to 4000 ft, and clear it for the ILS. It turns in when it reaches the line.

Points: landing +10, hand-off +10, wrong exit -5, go-around -5, arrival lost -10, loss of separation -20.
